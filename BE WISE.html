BE WISE: THE RIVER

“Every Action Has a Consequence.”

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D move · Mouse look (click the game to capture the mouse; if blocked, drag to look)
E interact, pick up waste, talk, clean the river
1–4 or click to choose an action.
Your morality score runs from −100 to +100. Reach either end to see your ending.

ABOUT

Be Wise: The River is an educational simulation about waste dumped into rivers. Small choices pile up into big consequences for the water, for health and for the people living beside it.

The river doesn't forget what you throw into it.
